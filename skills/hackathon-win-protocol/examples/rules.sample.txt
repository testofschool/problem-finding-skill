SYNTHETIC SAMPLE RULES PAGE (not a real contest)

Why we run this: We want small clinics to adopt the ExampleCo scheduling API in daily work.
Who decides: Finalists are chosen by a panel of five judges. Public votes are shown but do not count.
Eligibility: Each team has one to four members. Entries must be built during the event window.

Judging criteria
Real-world usefulness (40%)
Use of the ExampleCo API (30%)
Clarity of the demo (30%)

Finals: Each finalist presents for 5 minutes followed by 3 minutes of questions. Decks are limited to 10 slides including the cover, PDF only.
